About

Painting since 2013. Still learning can control.

Deco started writing on freight cars as a teenager, the way a lot of writers do, before moving into legal commissioned work in the early 2020s. The letterforms are the same either way — what changed was who was paying for the paint.

Every piece still starts as a black-book sketch before it touches a wall. The wall version always drifts from the sketch a little — that drift is usually the best part.
