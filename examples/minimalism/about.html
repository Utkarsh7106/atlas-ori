About

Independent practice, Helsinki, since 2016.

I design objects by taking things away. A chair, a lamp, a set of dishes — each starts as a longer list of parts, and the work is deciding which ones the object can live without.

Before starting the practice I spent six years in-house at two furniture manufacturers, which is where the discipline came from: every part you add has to be tooled, shipped, and eventually repaired by someone who isn't you.

Take away, don't add

The first version is never the reduced one. Reduction is a second and third pass, not a starting instinct.

Material tells the truth

A surface should look like what it's made of. No printed woodgrain, no faux finish.

Design for repair

If a part can't be replaced without replacing the whole object, it wasn't designed — it was assembled.
