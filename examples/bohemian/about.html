The Studio

About

Sela Ruiz grew up in a family of potters outside Taos, and spent a decade learning natural dyeing and floor-loom weaving from relatives before opening her own studio in 2014. Both crafts stayed in the same building on purpose — clay and cloth, she says, teach the same patience.

Every piece is dated and signed on the underside, and no glaze or dye recipe is ever repeated exactly, since the ash, minerals and plants she works from change slightly with every season.
