About

Lisbon. Painting since 2014.

I paint the way a dream actually works: everything rendered carefully enough to believe, arranged in a way that could never happen. A shadow that falls the wrong direction bothers people more than a shadow that isn't there at all.

Most of my commissioned work is editorial and album covers — briefs that want a single unsettling idea rendered with total conviction, not a collage of many strange things at once.
