About

* Montreal. Making games solo since 2018.

I worked as a level designer at two mid-size studios before going solo, mostly because the ideas I actually wanted to make kept getting cut in scoping meetings. Going independent meant I finally got to ship the weird ones.

Everything here is built in a small custom engine, drawn frame by frame, and released without a publisher. Slower, but it's mine.
