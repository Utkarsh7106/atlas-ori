About

Freelance, Austin TX, since 2019.

I design product interfaces for teams past the "let's just get something out" stage but before they have a full-time design hire. Usually that means one flow, one system, done properly, handed off as something an engineer can actually build without guessing.

Before freelancing I was the second designer at a logistics startup, which is where I learned that a dashboard used by people who didn't choose to use it has different rules than one people opted into.

Tools
Figma for design, a shared Storybook instance for handoff.
Engagement
