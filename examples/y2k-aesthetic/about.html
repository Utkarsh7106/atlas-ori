Our Story

A small studio making the internet shiny again.

Frostbyte started as a bedroom project in 2024, after founder Wren Ito found a box of old iMac G3 parts at a flea market and couldn't stop thinking about the plastic. Everything since has been an attempt to get that exact translucent gloss back onto things people actually carry around.

Every piece is moulded and finished by a small run of partner workshops — nothing is mass-produced past a few hundred units at a time, so colourways come and go.
