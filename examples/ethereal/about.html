About

June Larkin

June trained as a percussionist before she trained as a facilitator, which is still the easiest way to explain why the sound bath sessions sound the way they do — built from actual musical listening, not a preset app track.

She started holding sessions in her living room in 2017, for friends who kept asking if they could bring someone. The living room stopped being big enough within a year.

Her approach is deliberately unhurried: no session is ever run exactly the same way twice, because no room full of people is ever breathing the same way twice either.

Come as you are.
