About

Rotterdam. Small projects, drawn by hand before anything else.

too much detail here?

I trained in Delft and spent five years at a larger practice before starting on my own in 2019. Most of what I do now is small — a house, a pavilion, an annex — which means I still draw everything myself, all the way through.

I keep the sketches. A finished building hides the arguments that got it there, and I'd rather the process stayed visible, at least to the people who hire me.
