About

Founded in Paris, 2016.

Founder

Solène Marchetti trained under two of the houses that built the current idea of "niche" fragrance, then left to make something smaller. Maison Solène releases one new scent a year, at most.

Every fragrance starts from a single accord and stays there — nothing is added later to make it read as more expensive than it is.

“If you can name three things in it, we haven't finished.”
