No. IV

The Collection

Four scents, in rotation. Two more rest each year while their accords are reconsidered.

I.

Cendre

Vetiver and smoked cedar, built around a single ember-grey accord. Worn low and close.

II.

Linge Blanc

Iris and clean musk — the smell of a room just after it was aired out.

III.

Ambre Nu

A bare amber, without the usual vanilla underneath it. Warmer than it first reads.

IV.

Second Peau

Sandalwood and skin musk, formulated to sit under other scent rather than compete with it.
