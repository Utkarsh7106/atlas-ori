About

Three generations of the same family, the same workshop.

Thistlewood Organs was founded in 1962 by a cabinetmaker who fell into organ repair almost by accident, fixing a stuck key in a village chapel and never quite leaving the trade after. His granddaughter now runs the workshop.

Every instrument that comes through the shop is voiced by ear, pipe by pipe, in the room it will actually live in — never in the workshop alone, since a room changes an organ's voice as much as the pipes do.
