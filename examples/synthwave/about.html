About

Kestra Vale

Kestra started making synthwave the way most of the genre's artists did: buying a used Juno-106 and never quite putting it down. Four years and three releases later, the whole setup is still analog, live sets included — no laptop on stage, ever.

Every track starts from a single arpeggio pattern recorded first take, then built outward. If the arpeggio isn't right, the track doesn't happen.
