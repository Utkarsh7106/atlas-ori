About

Freelance, based in Toronto. Filing from wherever the story is.

Priya Kapoor has spent eleven years reporting on the people left holding the actual, physical consequences of decisions made somewhere else — growers, dockworkers, water managers, the last shift at a closing plant.

Her work has run in a handful of outlets that still fund a three-week reporting trip for a single piece, which she counts among her better career decisions.

She works slowly, on purpose. A dispatch takes as long as the story needs, not as long as an editorial calendar allows.
