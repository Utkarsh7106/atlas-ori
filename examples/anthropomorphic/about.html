About Pocket

Started by two people tired of budgeting apps that guilt-trip you.

We both spent years designing budgeting tools at banks, and every one of them was built to make you feel bad the second you overspent. Pocket started as a weekend project to see if a budgeting app could feel more like a friend keeping you company than an auditor.

The cast came before the charts. We built Pocket, Finch, Marlow and Wren first, and only added the actual budgeting math once we knew who'd be delivering it.
