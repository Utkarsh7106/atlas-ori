About

Trained in Kyoto, working there still.

Rin trained under a kintsugi master for six years before taking on repairs independently, and still sends difficult pieces back to that same workshop when a break is beyond what she trusts herself with alone.

A repair usually takes longer than the piece itself took to make — weeks of lacquer curing between each stage, gold applied only at the very end. There is no shortcut version; a fast kintsugi repair is usually just a gold-painted crack, not an actual one.
