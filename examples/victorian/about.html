About

A shop that has changed hands four times and its press not once.

The Corvid & Quill opened its doors in 1874 as a jobbing printer, taking whatever work came through — playbills, trade cards, the occasional broadside for a travelling show. The press itself, a hand-fed iron cylinder machine, has never left the shop floor.

Four proprietors later, the work has narrowed to stationery and invitations, but the method hasn't changed: type is still set by hand, one line at a time, from a case of wood and metal founts kept in the back room.
