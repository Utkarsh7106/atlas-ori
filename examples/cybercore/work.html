WORK

Five tools, three of them at v1.0, two still pre-release.

gitwatch

stable

Watches a repo and runs a command on every commit, using inotify instead of polling — near-zero overhead even on huge monorepos.

envdiff

stable

Diffs two .env files and flags anything missing, renamed, or accidentally left in plaintext before you deploy.

pgtail

stable

Tails a Postgres slow-query log in real time and formats it into something you can actually read at 2am.

portwatch

beta

Alerts you the moment a local dev port gets bound by something you didn't start yourself.

cronlint

pre-release

Catches cron expressions that will silently never fire, before they ship to production.
