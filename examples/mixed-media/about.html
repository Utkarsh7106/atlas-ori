About

Three inks, one screen, a lot of tape.

Nova Riot Press started in a converted garage in 2017 with a single secondhand Risograph and a stack of borrowed newsprint. The garage is now an actual studio, but the machine is the same one, patched twice.

Every job runs through the same constraint on purpose: three plates, one halftone screen, whatever stock is on the shelf that week. The limits are the whole point — infinite color would just make everything look like a regular printer.
