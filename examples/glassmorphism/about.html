About

Toronto. Composing and producing since 2015.

I trained as a classical cellist before I trained as a producer, which mostly shows up in how I think about arrangement — where an electronic track earns its low end the way an orchestra earns its bass section, not just by turning a knob.

Most of my work splits between scoring for picture and production for independent artists. I take a limited number of projects at a time so nothing gets a rushed mix.
