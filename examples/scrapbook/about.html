About

Keeping this journal since a very cold layover in 2016.

I started keeping a paper journal on a delayed flight in 2016 and never really stopped. Everything here is scanned from the real notebooks — the coffee rings are original.

I'm not a professional photographer or a travel writer by trade. This is just the record I'd have wanted to read before I left, kept in the hope someone else finds it useful, or at least kills an hour.

Kit

One notebook, one glue stick, a point-and-shoot film camera, no phone photos allowed in the book.

Rule

Every entry gets written the same day, on the actual page, not typed up later from memory.
